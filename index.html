    How the verdict works

    Each window counts the games where the player went over (or under) your line. Pushes don't count. The blended score weights recent form most: last 10 (35%), this season (25%), last 5 (20%), last season (10%) and vs. the next opponent (10%). Windows with no games are skipped.
