    How the verdict works

    Each window's hit rate counts the games the player went over (or under) your line. Pushes don't count. The verdict blends them, weighted toward recent form: last 10 (35%), this season (25%), last 5 (20%), last season (10%) and vs. the next opponent (10%). Windows with no games are skipped.

    Good means a blended rate of 65% or better. Lean means 55 to 64%. Pass means under 55%. Game logs don't know about injuries, minutes changes or who's in the lineup, so check the news before you bet.
